About me

Driven by an insatiable curiosity for the cosmos, I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Arizona mentored by Professor Ewan Douglas, dedicated to unveiling distant worlds through pioneering high-contrast direct imaging and demystifying the exozodiacal dust that surrounds them. My journey is built on an interdisciplinary foundation—holding a Master's in Physics from the University of New Mexico alongside double Bachelor's degrees in Electrical Engineering and Physics (minor in Mathematics) from National Taiwan University—empowering me to tackle complex astrophysical challenges with computational rigor and innovative problem-solving. I thrive at the frontier of discovery, passionately translating complex data into scientific insights to uncover the origins of life and the fundamental laws of nature.

What I'm interested in

  • Planet Formation

  • Space Telescope
